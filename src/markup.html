How bugs move the Health Score

Live in production, reviewed 14 Sep 2026. Score runs 0 to 10 and starts at 5.0. Recomputed nightly at 01:10 UTC by n8n from Jira EP, Hyve and IMP, written to four Planhat company fields.
-1.5
Incident Score · did production fail on this client?
IMP incidents weighted by severity, 180-day half-life, 24-month window. Value 0 to 8 maps linearly to 0 to -1.5. Two fresh critical incidents already hit the cap.
-1.0
Open Client Bug Weight · what is broken right now?
Weighted sum of currently open client-reported bugs (Urgent 3, High 2, Medium 1, Low 0; no priority counts as Medium). No decay: falls the moment a bug closes. Value 0 to 10 maps to 0 to -1.0.
-1.0
Client Bug Score · how much bug pain over two years?
Decayed weighted history of client-reported bugs, open and closed, same 180-day half-life. Value 0 to 40 maps to 0 to -1.0.
Together the three factors can remove at most -3.5. Anything raised before the client's most recent Won deal counts at 25%. Formula must stay linear, not linear (bounded), or the worst accounts score clean.

Which client gets the bug

The signal is "Reported by", not "Affected clients". Resolution order:
  1. Every Reported-by value INTERNAL: discard, not a client experience.
  2. A label with an override mapping: use those companies only.
  3. Otherwise Planhat's own companyIds on the synced Issue.
  4. Otherwise map each label through the Reported-by Label Dictionary to a Pipedrive company id, then to the Planhat company.

A ticket with several Reported-by values counts in full against every one of them, so one platform bug penalises ten accounts identically. If several accounts move together, check that first.

Which client gets the story

New here, and much weaker than the bug side:
  1. A story counts for a client only if it carries that client's label in Jira. 61 of 650 stories do.
  2. Labels are joined to Planhat companies through the same Pipedrive id dictionary the bug side uses, learned from how Bug Radar already resolved those labels. A3, Farnborough and Cosmoprof had no bug-side precedent and were mapped by name.
  3. INTERNAL-labelled and unlabelled stories become the platform band. They are never attributed to a client, and never counted in a client's Shipped column.

So "0 shipped" means nothing was recorded against their name, not that nothing reached them. Treat the Investment column as a question to ask, not an answer — and never net it against liability.

Operational traps

  1. Planhat returns HTTP 200 for a field name that does not exist. Renaming any of the four fields in Planhat silently freezes every company on yesterday's value.
  2. Closure is detected from the status name, not statusCategory. A new "closed" status that matches none of the known substrings keeps its bugs counted as open.
  3. Caps and weights live in three places: the Planhat Health Score Profile, the n8n Compute Health Factors node, and the Weekly Digest QUALITY array.
  4. Story summaries are AI-written from the Jira description and any linked spec. 92 of 650 are flagged low confidence and marked as such wherever they appear.

What this page is

A client-side merge of two existing dashboards — the Client Bug Radar (17 Sep) and the Delivery Timeline (16 Sep). Both originals are untouched; this page holds a copy of their data and reorganises it around the client rather than the team. Jira, Confluence, the workbook and Planhat all pull through connectors; only the 2026 story feed is still re-inherited by hand. The pipeline ledger lives in this artifact’s shared database, the logic registry in logic.json and the flow extract in flow.json: every pull is stored once and read, never re-run on page load.

Jira EP changelog (flow)

64 EP stories and bugs sampled 22 Sep 2026 (50 done, 14 in flight): every status transition and the time in each state, read with expand=changelog through the Atlassian connector and stored in flow.json. It is a sample; the full pull of about 1,200 issues is a scheduled batch. The Flow tab reads the file as it is.

Pipeline ledger

18 must-have items held in this artifact’s shared database, schema 2 since 22 Sep 2026: Team from the Jira ticket, Driver (who it is for), a one-line Why with the old text kept as Rationale. Jira status, due date and logged time are read per row and dated.
