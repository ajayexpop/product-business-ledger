## 'What we built at your request' (DLG section)

- 2023-Q2 **Spam protection for exhibitor registration**: You asked for protection after spam sign-ups forced exhibitor registration to be switched off. Exhibitor registration now includes reCAPTCHA, so it can stay open.  
  `EP-16297`
- 2023-Q3 **Ticket order you control**: Tickets now keep the order you set in the registration pipeline, even after you edit or add participant categories.  
  `EP-17102`
- 2024-Q2 **Aditus badges inside the app**: With one setting, the app shows the custom badge page from Aditus, your registration partner, instead of the standard badge. Attendees scan the badge they actually registered with.  
  `EP-25790`
- 2024-Q3 **Lead downloads by exhibitor category**: Switch lead downloads on or off for a whole exhibitor category or for a single exhibitor, instead of for every exhibitor at once.  
  `EP-13663`
- 2024-Q3 **Security questions for exhibitor events**: Set your own questions that exhibitors must answer when they submit an event, and read the answers in the admin panel before you approve.  
  `EP-26093`
- 2024-Q3 **Product categories as session filters**: Visitors can filter sessions by product category, with the same filter setup you already use for exhibitor events.  
  `EP-29933`
- 2024-Q3 **Activity categories only for exhibitors**: A registration setting restricts exhibitors to choosing from your activity categories when they add product categories.  
  `EP-33961`
- 2024-Q3 **Sponsor pop-up through the API**: Your exhibitor data feed can now switch an exhibitor's sponsor pop-up on or off through the API, with no manual edits in the admin panel.  
  `EP-32201`
- 2024-Q4 **Admin notifications in every language**: Write each admin notification once per language and every visitor gets it in their own language, including visitors who are not logged in.  
  `EP-31011 EP-32750`
- 2024-Q4 **App pages for visitors who are not logged in**: Show selected app pages only to visitors who are not logged in, and link them straight to the right sign-in page from your custom content.  
  `EP-32358 EP-32359`
- 2024-Q4 **Faster sponsor administration**: The sponsor page in the admin panel, which you reported as slow, has been reworked to load faster.  
  `EP-36019`
- 2025-Q2 **German across the platform**: German wording corrected and completed across the platform, using EuroTier 2024 as the reference and applied to every event.  
  `EP-40706 EP-41169`
- 2025-Q3 **Tax names in every language**: Tax names on invoices and at checkout now follow the buyer's language, like the rest of registration.  
  `EP-43163`
- 2025-Q3 **Aditus badge settings restored**: The Aditus custom badge setting is back in the admin panel, so you can manage it yourselves event by event.  
  `EP-44694`
- 2026-Q1 **Favourites and floor plan bookmarks in sync**: Favouriting an exhibitor bookmarks their stand on the floor plan, and bookmarking a stand favourites the exhibitor, so visitors plan their visit once.  
  `EP-44186`
- 2026-Q1 **Clearer badge messages with Aditus**: When a custom badge can't be loaded, visitors now see a clear explanation of what happened and what to do, instead of being sent silently to a different badge page.  
  `EP-48297`
- 2026-Q1 **Safer floor plan updates between web and app**: After Agritechnica 2025, when an ExpoFP update reached the app before the web, we added a way to switch the floor plan source so web and app stay in step.  
  `EP-47929`
- 2026-Q2 **Hall and stand management for large events**: Hall and stand lists in the floor plan admin sort naturally and let you select and remove many at once, built with Agritechnica's many halls in mind.  
  `EP-51701`
- 2026-Q2 **Past events stay available in the app**: Visitors can reopen finished events in the app and still find their data. The fix followed a sync gap seen after Agritechnica 2025.  
  `EP-52820`
- 2026-Q3 **Private favourites for opted-out visitors**: Visitors who opt out of networking can still favourite exhibitors, visitors and products on web and in the app. Their favourites stay private, in line with GDPR.  
  `EP-53978 EP-57577`
- 2026-Q3 **Brands attached across events on import**: Exhibitor imports can attach existing brands across your events, so brands don't have to be rebuilt for each show.  
  `EP-55642`
- 2026-Q3 **Custom session filters in the API**: Set and read your custom session filters through the Sessions API, with filter titles returned in the requested language.  
  `EP-57347 EP-57348`
- 2026-Q3 **Dedicated SAML single sign-on page**: At your urgent request, SAML single sign-on now has its own settings page in the integrations catalogue.  
  `EP-57684`
